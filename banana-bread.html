Stella's Banana Bread

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

Serves
10 people
Prep
20 min
Cook
1 hour

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter, room temperature
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • Bourbon vanilla, to taste
  • Crushed nuts (almonds, nutmeg, ginger noted)
  • A dash of salt
  • Cardamom, to taste

Directions

  1. In a bowl, mix and combine the dry ingredients: flour, almond flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon/cardamom and a dash of salt.
  2. Add sugar to the room-temperature butter and whip for about 2 minutes.
  3. Add olive oil, then add the room-temperature eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mash the bananas and add them to the wet mixture.
  5. Add the wet mixture to the dry ingredients and combine. Fold in nuts if using.
  6. Bake for about 1 hour at 350°F.

Stella's Notes 💕

Use room-temperature butter and eggs. Add a handful of chopped nuts to the batter, and/or top with rolled oats.
