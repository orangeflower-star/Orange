Cozy Immune Tea

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

🍊 🍋 🫚 🍯 ☕ ♡
Serves
2-3
Prep
30-45 min
Cook
30-45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole ginger root
  • 2-4 garlic cloves
  • 2 oranges
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 limes or lemons
  • 2-3 cups water

Directions

  1. Peel and grate the ginger into a clean pot.
  2. Grate the garlic.
  3. Thinly slice the orange and lime/lemon and add them to the pot with the skin on.
  4. Add the cinnamon stick and water. Cover and simmer for about 10 minutes.
  5. Bring to high heat for 3-5 minutes, then reduce the heat and let it simmer/steep for another 15-20 minutes.
  6. Serve warm.

Stella's Notes 💕

Handwritten note: wash fruit before use; vinegar or baking soda was suggested for washing.
