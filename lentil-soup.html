Cozy Lentil Soup

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

🥣 🌶️ 🥄 🍅 🌿 ♡
Serves
6
Prep
15 min
Cook
30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups lentils
  • 1 whole onion
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup chopped parsley
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 1 thumb-size piece ginger
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup concentrated bone broth
  • 2 tbsp coconut aminos
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1 dash cayenne pepper
  • 1 tsp pepper flakes
  • 1 habanero pepper
  • 1 tbsp pepper-soup seasoning
  • Optional extra spices: oregano, rosemary, curry powder, cumin, chili powder

Directions

  1. Toast cayenne pepper briefly in olive oil. Add onion, garlic, ginger and habanero; sauté for about 2 minutes.
  2. Add mushrooms, bone broth and spices. Sauté for about 3 minutes.
  3. Add washed lentils and about 3 cups water.
  4. Cook on high for about 10 minutes, then reduce to medium heat for about 15 minutes, checking and stirring occasionally.
  5. Add maple syrup, coconut aminos, pepper flakes and salt to taste. Simmer for about 5 minutes.
  6. Add cherry tomatoes and parsley for the final 2 minutes. Cover, remove from heat and let rest for about 5 minutes.

Stella's Notes 💕

Best served with garlic naan. Add more water if needed and cook until the lentils are done.
