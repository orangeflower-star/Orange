Wellness Is Not Always Linear 🍂

Fall ’26 Edition

It’s officially October, the weather is changing, and everything around us is changing too. The leaves are turning colors, and fall brings with it a lot of good things: pumpkin carving, apple cider drinks, apple picking, cute stories, and lots of cozy memories.

But it can also be a hard time for a lot of people.

With the sun setting sooner, the colder weather, and those end-of-year plans that haven't been met, it's understandable why some people struggle with depression around this time of year. I totally get it.

For college students, it's peak time for studying. Midterms might be coming up, and the pressure starts to kick in. For working professionals, closing deals or meeting deadlines are fast approaching. It can be an overwhelming time.

And let's not even talk about the loneliness epidemic yet.

Thanksgiving is around the corner, and the folks might be expecting a special someone at dinner. Grandma might think you have a girl or a guy, and just the thought of letting them down once again... I get it.

This season comes with so many blissful events that it can make it almost impossible for the lonely at heart to find true happiness in the midst of it all.

But there are a few things you can do to stay positive-ish.

I know, I know, but hear me out first. 💕

Appreciate the Little Things

Appreciate every hello, hi, and "How are you doing?"

It sounds silly when you feel like your world is falling apart to be grateful for a simple hello, but try to see the positive in those little moments first.

Sometimes, it's the smallest things that make a difference.

Make a Fall/Autumn Bucket List 🍁

Make a fall or autumn bucket list! I'll include a free printable PDF on the Resources page. Visit the Resources page →

But keep it short, maybe five things, so you don't feel overwhelmed.

The goal isn't to add more pressure to your life. It's simply to have a few things to look forward to.

Reach Out to Your People

Reach out to someone who always has a way of making you happy on a bad day.

You know those people who bring just pure positive vibes? These people are angels among us, lol.

Sometimes, a conversation with the right person can make your day feel a little lighter.

Read a Book, Try Something New, or Take Time for Yourself

Read a book. Take 30 minutes each day for yourself and do something fun.

Try something new. Maybe even study a little harder.

I have a regression class, and honestly, I am a little nervous. So, for 15 minutes each day, I've been trying to quiz myself on a few materials.

Let's just say that hasn't been going great, but I think I might be getting better, lol.

The point is to keep trying, even when things aren't going perfectly.

Plan Little Moments to Look Forward To ☕️

I have personally tried scheduling a bubble bath, a long walk, or a movie night.

You can even text a friend and ask if you can call them. Plan these things in advance, so you have something to look forward to.

I also love a good sunrise lamp. I've used one for as long as I can remember, especially during this time of year.

And a good tea or hot cocoa? Always a yes.

Sometimes, taking the time to enjoy these little things does wonders.

Keep Trying, Even When It's Hard

Life is all about trying and never giving up.

And when you want to quit, or you feel like all hope is lost, please talk to a healthcare provider.

And if you can't afford that, reach out to someone who will listen.

You don't have to keep everything to yourself.

Wellness is not always linear. Some days will feel better than others, and that's okay. You can enjoy the beautiful things fall has to offer while still having days when everything feels a little overwhelming.

Keep trying, keep finding little things to look forward to, and remember that you don't have to have everything figured out before the year ends.

With love,

Stella 🍊💕
