Stella's Homemade Bread

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

🍞 🥣 🧈 🌾 ♡
Serves
5-7 people
Prep
About 3 hours (including rise time)
Cook
35 min

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 packet active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup milk

Directions

  1. Activate the yeast: in a small bowl, add yeast, water, milk and sugar. Stir, cover and leave for 10-15 minutes.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the flour and salt. Make a well in the center and pour in the activated yeast mixture and olive oil. Stir until a dough forms.
  3. Knead the dough for about 20 minutes by hand, adding a little flour, water or oil as needed. The dough should become smooth and elastic.
  4. Cover and let rise for 1-2 hours, or until doubled in size.
  5. Punch down the dough and shape into a loaf.
  6. Bake at 350°F for about 35 minutes.

Stella's Notes 💕

The handwritten page emphasizes adjusting flour, water or oil while kneading until the dough feels smooth and elastic.
