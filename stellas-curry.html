Stella's Cozy Curry

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

🌶️ 🍆 🥘 🌿 🥄 ♡
Serves
10-12 people
Prep
10-15 min
Cook
45-50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 large eggplant
  • 1 whole onion
  • 2 bell peppers (colored)
  • 6 oz baby corn
  • 1 oz dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 4 small red potatoes
  • 1 large piece ginger
  • 1 handful basil
  • 1 cup mixed nuts (almonds, cashews, hazelnuts and pecans; unsalted)
  • Beef chunks
  • 1 curry stick/block
  • Cinnamon, thyme and mushroom seasoning
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp each cumin, oregano and cardamom
  • About 1 cup broth + 1 cup water, as needed
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Salt, to taste

Directions

  1. Wash the eggplant and cut into small cubes. Soak the dried mushrooms in salted water to rehydrate.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the meat with about 1/4 tsp baking soda and spices, including ginger and garlic. Add water and cook until the meat is tender.
  3. Once the cooking liquid has reduced, add ground mixed nuts; the mixture will thicken quickly.
  4. Add onion and habanero/bell peppers and sauté briefly. Add broth and water, then season to taste.
  5. Add sugar, salt, potatoes, mushrooms and eggplant. Cook for about 15-20 minutes over medium heat.
  6. After about 15 minutes, add baby corn. Cook about 10 minutes more, adding bell peppers in the final 2 minutes.
  7. Add basil and remove from the stove.

Stella's Notes 💕

Adjust everything to taste. The handwritten recipe encourages adding more of what you like.
