Behind Orange Flower

Hi, welcome to my little corner of the internet.

Orange Flower is an extension of the content I love creating online — lifestyle, nutrition, wellness, and conversations about navigating life as a Gen Z woman.

I'm especially interested in public health and nutrition, but I also love the everyday side of life: routines, organization, self-growth, beauty, lifestyle, and learning how to make our 20s feel a little less confusing.

My goal is to create a space that feels useful, relatable, educational, and fun.

My podcast

🎙️ Gen Z in their 20s

I started my podcast, Gen Z in their 20s by Lulu Musik, during my undergraduate years. It's a space for conversations about navigating your 20s, personal growth, relationships, mental wellness, culture, and life as part of Gen Z.

I haven't recorded a new season recently, but it's still part of my story — and maybe it's time for a comeback.
