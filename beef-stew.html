Cozy Beef Stew

A cozy little recipe from my kitchen to yours. 💕

🥕 🥔 🥘 🌿 🥄 ♡
Serves
5-8
Prep
15 min
Cook
About 1 hr 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1-2 lb beef
  • 2-3 cups red wine
  • 4 large carrots
  • Mini potatoes
  • 1 1/2 onions
  • Sliced mushrooms
  • Minced garlic
  • 3 large celery stalks
  • 2 cups peas
  • Cornstarch or flour
  • 2-3 cups beef broth
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Parsley
  • Bay leaves (2)
  • Seasonings noted: chili powder, cumin, salt, black pepper, curry powder, cardamom powder, red pepper flakes
  • Optional steak seasoning

Directions

  1. Marinate the beef overnight with your preferred seasonings. The notes suggest a little baking soda to help tenderize.
  2. Using a little oil, brown the beef on all sides for about 5 minutes, then set aside.
  3. In the same pot, add onion, mushrooms, garlic and carrots. Stir-fry for about 5 minutes.
  4. Deglaze the pot with red wine. Return the meat, add bay leaves and seasonings, then add beef broth. Cover and simmer on low-medium heat for about 20 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes halfway through, adding more beef broth as needed.
  6. Add celery and cook for about 10 minutes. Add peas.
  7. For a thicker stew, stir in about 2 tbsp flour mixed with water (cornstarch can also be used).
  8. Add parsley, cook about 5 minutes more, then cover and let stand 5-10 minutes.

Stella's Notes 💕

The original page says to season to taste and notes that a combination of soy sauce and coconut aminos can be used.
